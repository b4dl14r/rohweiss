Contact form: purpose and legal basis

When you write to us through the contact form, we process your name, your email address and your message, and, if you provide them, your phone number, company and topic, for partner enquiries your field and portfolio address, as well as the language of the page. The purpose is to handle your enquiry and reply to you. The legal basis is your consent under Art. 6 (1) (a) GDPR, which you give by ticking the box in the form, and, where a contract or its preparation is concerned, Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR. You can withdraw your consent at any time with effect for the future. The message is delivered by email to the rohweiss mailbox at the host. It is not passed on to any further third parties.
